ABOUT

Founded in 2025 in the heart of Salem, Tamil Nadu, MAGIZH NUTS was born from a family's promise to bring nature's finest to your table. What began as a small family venture is now a commitment to trust, quality, and pure goodness. We hand-select only the best cashews, almonds, pistachios, and walnuts, working closely with local farmers. Every nut is cleaned, quality-checked, and packed with love - because for us, it's more than nuts. It's about trust you can taste.

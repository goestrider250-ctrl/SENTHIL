The Kodaikanal Trip – An Unforgettable Journey

It all started from Thoothukudi. We planned a trip to Kodaikanal with our friends, and after making all the arrangements, we started our journey at night. Before leaving, we went to SPIC, gathered all the girls, and around 12 midnight, we finally started our trip. Since I was the only one with the girls at the bus stand, I had to bring everyone along. One of my friends had already started drinking, so I went to the girls and made sure everyone was ready. After that, all the boys joined us, and finally, our Kodaikanal journey began. The journey itself was amazing. As we travelled through the hills, the weather became cooler and the climate was simply awesome. We enjoyed the road trip, talked, laughed, and somehow managed to reach Kodaikanal around 8 in the morning. After reaching our room, we freshened up and started exploring Kodaikanal. First, we went boating and enjoyed the beautiful surroundings. Then we visited Pine Forest, clicked lots of pictures, and enjoyed the peaceful atmosphere. After that, we went to Guna Caves. While returning from Guna Caves, something unexpected happened. One of my friends fell down from his bike! Luckily, he didn't get seriously injured, but the bike had some damage. Somehow, he then got into an argument with a car owner. After everything was settled, we continued our journey. Then came another comedy scene. My friend had lost one of his slippers, so he started riding the bike with just one slipper. We all started scolding him and told him to remove it and buy a new pair. When we went to buy slippers, the shopkeeper initially asked for ₹800. After some bargaining, we finally managed to get them for ₹500. 😂 After that, we continued exploring Kodaikanal, enjoyed the places, clicked more pictures, and finally returned to our room. We were completely tired, so we went to sleep. The next morning, we went for a jeep ride and enjoyed the hills and the beautiful views one last time. By evening, we started our return journey to Thoothukudi. But our trip wasn't complete without one final adventure. On the way back, our friends kept changing the route on the map and confusing everyone. 😂 Somehow, after taking different routes and making the journey even more adventurous, we finally reached Thoothukudi safely. We dropped all the girls safely at their homes and then we boys headed back to our own houses. It was a simple trip, but the unexpected incidents, funny moments, beautiful places, and the memories we created together made it one of the most unforgettable trips of our lives. ❤️‍🔥
